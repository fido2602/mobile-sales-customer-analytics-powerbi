MOBILE PHONE SALES ANALYTICS — DATA METADATA
==============================================

Source workbook:
Day - 30 - Mobile Sales Data.xlsx

Main source sheet:
Sheet1

Source rows: 3,835
Source columns: 16
Observed date range: 2021-10-09 to 2024-10-08

CORE FIELDS
-----------
Transaction ID     Unique transaction identifier in the supplied data.
Day                Day of month.
Month              Numeric month.
Year               Calendar year.
Day Name           Day-of-week label. Abbreviated values in the source are standardized in the portfolio fact table.
Brand              Mobile-phone brand.
Units Sold         Quantity sold in the transaction.
Price Per Unit     Price for one unit.
Customer Name      Customer name field supplied in the source.
Customer Age       Customer age field supplied in the source.
City               Customer/transaction city field supplied in the source.
Payment Method     Payment method.
Customer Ratings   Rating value supplied in the source.
Mobile Model       Mobile phone model.

DERIVED FIELDS
--------------
Date               Constructed from Year + Month + Day.
Total Sales        Units Sold * Price Per Unit.
Year_Month         YYYY-MM analytical date label in dim_date.

CORE KPIs
---------
Total Sales = SUM(Total Sales)
Total Units = SUM(Units Sold)
Transactions = DISTINCTCOUNT(Transaction ID)
Average Price per Unit = AVERAGE(Price Per Unit)
Average Sales per Transaction = AVERAGE(Total Sales)

DATA MODEL
----------
fact_mobile_sales is the transaction-level fact table.
dim_date supports time analysis.
dim_brands supports brand filtering.
dim_cities supports geographic filtering.
dim_payment_methods supports payment analysis.
dim_mobile_models supports model analysis.

QUALITY NOTES
-------------
- The source contains both full day names and a small number of abbreviated day names; abbreviations are standardized.
- Customer names repeat and customer age is not stable for every repeated name, so Customer Age is retained as a transaction-level attribute rather than treated as a slowly changing customer dimension.
- 2021 and 2024 are partial years in the supplied data.
- The dashboard KPI labeled "Average" matches the average Price Per Unit calculation (approximately $40.11K).
- Sales are calculated as Units Sold multiplied by Price Per Unit.
